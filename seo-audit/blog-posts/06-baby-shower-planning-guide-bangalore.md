A baby shower in Bangalore should ideally be planned 6–8 weeks in advance, held during the 7th or 8th month of pregnancy, and kept intimate — typically 20–50 guests — with a clear theme, light catering, and enough games to keep the energy warm without exhausting the mother-to-be.

Baby showers are growing rapidly across Bangalore's young professional communities, blending Indian traditions (seemantham, valaikappu) with the more Western-styled baby shower format. Here is a comprehensive, week-by-week planning guide based on Eevagga's experience coordinating baby showers across the city.

## Week 1–2: Set the Date, Guest List, and Venue

**Timing:** The 7th or 8th month is the sweet spot — the mother-to-be is visibly expecting (beautiful for photos), still comfortable enough to celebrate, and far enough from the due date to avoid logistical stress.

**Guest count:** Baby showers in Bangalore typically range from 20–50 guests. Beyond 50, the intimate warmth of the occasion starts to feel more like a large party. Keep it close — family, close friends, and colleagues who genuinely matter.

**Venue options:**

| Venue Type | Good For | Cost Range |
|---|---|---|
| Home / terrace | Maximum intimacy, most flexibility on décor | ₹0 (your space) + décor |
| Restaurant private dining | Catering handled, stylish setting, minimal setup effort | ₹800–₹2,000 per head (min pax usually applies) |
| Apartment clubhouse / community hall | Affordable, familiar for guests | ₹3,000–₹15,000 to hire |
| Boutique event space | Professional, photogenic, premium experience | ₹15,000–₹50,000 for the space |

**Important:** If using an apartment clubhouse, confirm the club's policy on external caterers, decoration permissions, and noise timing before booking.

---

## Week 2–3: Fix the Theme

A clear theme simplifies every subsequent decision — the decorator knows what to source, the cake baker knows what to design, and the photographer knows what aesthetic to shoot for.

**Top baby shower themes in Bangalore (2026):**

- **Boho Wildflowers:** Earthy, warm, and photogenic. Dried pampas grass, wildflower arrangements, macramé accents. Works beautifully for home and garden settings.
- **Twinkle Twinkle Little Star:** Classic, gender-neutral, soft gold and white palette. Widely popular for families who have not found out the baby's gender.
- **Oh Baby Botanical:** Lush greenery, fresh flowers, white and green palette. Clean, modern, and elegant.
- **Jungle Animals:** Bright, cheerful, perfect when the gender is known and the family has a more playful style.
- **Pastel Rainbow:** Soft rainbow arches, cloud balloons, pastel shades. Works for both gender reveals and standard baby showers.
- **Mommy to Bee:** Yellow and black bee theme — sweet, whimsical, popular for outdoor setups.

**Gender-neutral themes** are increasingly the default in Bangalore's millennial parent community, where gender reveal showers are growing in popularity but many families still prefer to keep the gender private.

## Week 3–4: Brief Your Decorator and Plan the Menu

**Decoration:** Share your theme references with your decorator and confirm the setup timeline. A standard baby shower decoration setup in Bangalore — backdrop, balloon garland, themed table, welcome board — costs ₹8,000–₹30,000 depending on floral content and backdrop complexity.

**Catering considerations for a baby shower:**
- Keep the menu light: finger foods, sandwiches, mini desserts, fruit platters
- Ensure at least two non-spicy, easily digestible options — the mother-to-be will be eating too
- Many Bangalore caterers offer dedicated baby shower menus at ₹600–₹1,200 per head
- Fresh fruit infused water or mocktails are a more thoughtful choice than sodas
- The cake should not be the only sweet — a dessert table with macarons, cake pops, and cupcakes is a standard feature

---

## Week 4–5: Organise Games and Photography

**Games that work at Bangalore baby showers:**
- **Baby Bingo:** Guests fill in a bingo card with gifts they think will be received; works best with 20+ guests
- **Guess the Baby Food:** Unlabelled baby food jars; guests guess the flavour — reliably funny
- **Mommy or Daddy?** Trivia questions about which parent will do what; involves everyone including the father-to-be
- **Belly Cast Activity:** A keepsake for the mother — plaster belly cast done during the shower with guests watching or participating. Needs advance materials.
- **Baby Shower Wishes Jar:** Guests write wishes or advice on cards to be read later — a quiet, meaningful activity that requires no organisation

**Photography:** A candid photographer for 2–3 hours is ideal. Rates in Bangalore range from ₹5,000–₹18,000. Confirm they have baby shower experience — the best moments (the expressions when gifts are opened, the bump photos, the games) require someone attuned to this event format.

---

## Week 5–6: Order Cake and Favours

**Cake:** Custom baby shower cakes from Bangalore's specialist bakers (concentrated in Indiranagar, Koramangala, and Jayanagar) start at ₹2,500 for a simple design and rise based on size and custom elements. Sculpted or novelty designs (like a baby bassinet or stork cake) require 10–14 days' notice.

**Favours:** Popular choices among Bangalore's baby shower hosts:
- Personalised scented candles with the baby's name or due date
- Seed kits (herb or flower seed packets — a living favour, aligned with botanical themes)
- Mini honey jars or jam jars with personalised labels
- Macaron boxes in theme colours
- Small potted succulents

---

## Week 7–8: Final Confirmation and Day-Of Timeline

Reconfirm with every vendor — decorator, caterer, photographer, cake baker — 5–7 days before. Get written or WhatsApp confirmations with the exact time and address.

**Day-of timeline sample:**

| Time | Activity |
|---|---|
| 3 hours before guests arrive | Decorator begins setup |
| 30 mins before | Final check: food stations, welcome table, seating |
| Guest arrival window | 30–45 minutes of arrival and settling |
| Activities begin | Games, conversation, gift opening |
| Cake cutting | Around the midpoint of the event |
| Photographer wraps | 2–3 hours in |
| Event close | Allow 30 mins for guests to linger after the formal activities end |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is the best time to have a baby shower in Bangalore?**
The 7th month (28th–32nd week of pregnancy) is the ideal time. The mother-to-be is comfortably expecting, visually beautiful for photos, and not yet at the stage where travel and long gatherings become tiring.

**What are the most popular baby shower themes in Bangalore?**
Boho Wildflowers, Twinkle Twinkle, Oh Baby Botanical, Jungle Animals, and Pastel Rainbow are the top themes in 2026. Gender-neutral themes are increasingly preferred.

**How much does a baby shower cost in Bangalore?**
For 30–50 guests, expect ₹45,000–₹1,20,000 for a fully planned baby shower covering venue, décor, catering, cake, and photography. The biggest cost variables are venue choice and catering quality.
